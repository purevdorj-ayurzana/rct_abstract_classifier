# Data rights

PubMed/MEDLINE is the source of the publication metadata and abstracts. We thank
the U.S. National Library of Medicine for making these records available.

The repository's MIT license applies to the pipeline code, not to the journal
abstracts. Abstracts and other third-party text retain their existing copyright
and licensing terms. Their inclusion does not grant additional reuse rights.

See [NLM copyright and downloading information](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/databases/download.html).

The original PubMed exports are preserved. Classifications and human annotations
are study outputs and are not annotations supplied by PubMed or NLM.
