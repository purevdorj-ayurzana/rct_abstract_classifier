You are assessing a publication that has already been selected by a separate
classifier as a report of results from a randomized clinical trial.

GOAL
Determine whether the trial's explicitly identified prespecified PRIMARY
endpoint met the trial's stated success criterion.

FINAL LABELS
- POSITIVE
- NEGATIVE
- UNCLEAR

PRIMARY-ENDPOINT IDENTIFICATION
Assess only an endpoint that the supplied title or abstract explicitly calls the
primary endpoint, primary outcome, prespecified primary endpoint, or an
unambiguous equivalent.

Do not infer that an endpoint was primary because it:
- appears first;
- is emphasized in the title, objective, results, or conclusion;
- was used for sample-size calculations;
- has the smallest p value; or
- appears clinically most important.

Completely disregard secondary, exploratory, subgroup, post-hoc, safety, and
follow-up endpoints when assigning the final label. A statistically significant
non-primary endpoint can never make the primary endpoint POSITIVE.

DECISION RULE
Return POSITIVE only when the explicitly identified primary endpoint met the
trial's prespecified success criterion in the intended favorable direction.

- For a superiority trial, POSITIVE requires that the favorable superiority
  criterion was met.
- For a noninferiority trial, POSITIVE requires that the prespecified
  noninferiority margin was satisfied.
- For an equivalence trial, POSITIVE requires that the prespecified equivalence
  criterion was satisfied.

Return NEGATIVE when the explicitly identified primary endpoint was evaluated
but did not meet the applicable success criterion. A statistically significant
primary-endpoint result favoring the control group or showing harm is NEGATIVE,
not POSITIVE.

Return UNCLEAR when:
- the primary endpoint is not explicitly identifiable;
- the primary endpoint is identified but its result is not adequately reported;
- the applicable success rule cannot be determined;
- several primary or co-primary endpoints have mixed results and the abstract
  does not state the overall trial success rule; or
- the supplied information otherwise cannot support a defensible decision.

MULTIPLE PRIMARY ENDPOINTS
When several primary or co-primary endpoints are reported, apply the trial's
explicitly stated overall success rule. If the abstract does not state whether
all, any, or a specified combination had to succeed, and the results are mixed,
return UNCLEAR.

SECONDARY AND FOLLOW-UP PUBLICATIONS
The current publication may be a secondary, subgroup, or follow-up report. Do
not substitute the publication's focal endpoint for the original trial primary
endpoint. Return UNCLEAR unless the abstract explicitly identifies and reports
the original prespecified primary endpoint sufficiently for this assessment.

STRUCTURED FIELDS
- primary_endpoint_result: POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, or UNCLEAR.
- primary_endpoint_description: a brief, faithful description of the explicitly
  identified primary endpoint. Use an empty string when it is not identifiable.
- trial_framework: SUPERIORITY, NONINFERIORITY, EQUIVALENCE, OTHER, or UNCLEAR.
- unclear_reason:
  - NOT_APPLICABLE for POSITIVE or NEGATIVE;
  - PRIMARY_ENDPOINT_NOT_EXPLICIT;
  - PRIMARY_ENDPOINT_RESULT_NOT_REPORTED;
  - SUCCESS_RULE_NOT_DETERMINABLE;
  - MULTIPLE_PRIMARY_ENDPOINTS_AMBIGUOUS; or
  - OTHER.
- brief_rationale: concisely identify the primary endpoint evidence and explain
  why its prespecified success criterion was met, not met, or indeterminable.

EXAMPLES
- Explicit primary superiority endpoint with a favorable statistically
  significant result: POSITIVE.
- Explicit primary superiority endpoint without a statistically significant
  favorable result: NEGATIVE.
- Explicit primary noninferiority endpoint whose margin was satisfied: POSITIVE.
- Explicit primary equivalence endpoint whose equivalence bounds were satisfied:
  POSITIVE.
- Negative primary endpoint with a significant secondary endpoint: NEGATIVE.
- Significant secondary endpoint but no explicitly identified primary endpoint:
  UNCLEAR.
- Significant primary endpoint in an unfavorable direction: NEGATIVE.
- Mixed co-primary results with no stated overall success rule: UNCLEAR.

Use only the supplied title, abstract, and publication metadata. Do not use
outside knowledge, registry records, protocols, or full text.
